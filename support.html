The scan says it can't read my pose

The measurement needs to see your whole body clearly. Try this: stand side-on to the camera, the full body in frame from head to feet, a plain wall behind you, even lighting, and fitted clothes so your back line is visible. Prop the phone up rather than holding it.

Where do my photos go?

Nowhere. The analysis runs on your iPhone and the photo is never uploaded. If you chose to keep it for before/after comparison it stays in the app's private storage on your device, and you can delete it any time from Profile.

How is the posture score calculated?

Four categories — head position, shoulders, upper back and pelvis — are each scored out of 100 from an angle measured in your photo, then averaged into your total. Open any scan from the Today screen to see each measured value next to its target.

It is an in-app indicator for comparing your own scans over time. It is not a clinical measurement and is not comparable to anyone else's score.

I can't do an exercise / it hurts

Use "Too hard" during a session — the plan will adjust. Never push through sharp pain, numbness or tingling. If a movement consistently hurts, stop and speak to a healthcare professional.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iOS Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Lumba → Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I restored my phone and lost my progress

Your scans and history live on your device, so a wipe removes them. Your subscription can be restored: open the paywall and tap "Restore Purchases".

How do I delete my data?

Profile → Delete all data removes everything on the device. To also remove the anonymous record on our servers, email us and mention the anonymous ID shown in Profile → About.

Lumba is not a medical device. It provides general exercise guidance, not medical advice or diagnosis. If you have severe or persistent pain, numbness, weakness, or a recent injury, please consult a healthcare professional.
