4. The posture score

The score is calculated from angles estimated by on-device pose detection in a single photo. It is affected by your clothing, camera angle, lighting and how you happened to stand. It is useful for comparing your own scans over time, and it is not comparable to a clinical assessment or to another person's score.

How it is calculated: four categories — head position, shoulders, upper back and pelvis — each scored out of 100 from a measured angle, then averaged into your total. The app shows you each measured value and its target so you can see exactly where the number comes from.
